## FAQ

**Will you guarantee a 90+ PageSpeed score?**
No — and be careful with anyone who does. Your score depends heavily on the apps and marketing scripts running on your store, which I do not control. What I guarantee is a documented method, measurements taken under identical conditions before and after, and a report you can hand to any other developer for verification.

**Will my store go down?**
No. I work on a duplicate, unpublished theme. Your live store is untouched until you review a preview link and give written approval. I also create a dated backup before starting, and you get a written rollback plan.

**Do you need full admin access?**
No. The Theme Access app gives me theme files only. If you want me to also read your performance reports and app list, I can request a collaborator account limited to Themes, Apps and Reports.

**Why is my Shopify score different from PageSpeed Insights?**
They measure different things. Your Shopify dashboard reports real visitor data over 30 days. PageSpeed Insights runs a simulated test on a virtual device. Both are useful; only the first reflects what your customers experience.

**Will you remove my apps?**
Only if you ask me to. I will tell you exactly what each app costs in page weight and where it loads, and recommend a course of action. The decision is yours and I document it.

**How soon will I see results in my Shopify dashboard?**
The dashboard uses a rolling 30-day window of real sessions and updates with a delay. Lab measurements are immediate; real-world data moves over several weeks. I include this timing in every report.

**What if something breaks?**
Every change is a separate, reversible commit. You get a rollback plan that restores the previous theme in under two minutes. I remain available during the follow-up window included in your package.

**Do you work with custom or premium themes?**
Yes. Send me the theme name before ordering and I will confirm.
