## FAQ

**Why not just use an app?**
Sometimes an app is the right answer, and I'll tell you when. Apps win when the feature needs server logic, when your needs will change a lot, or when the app costs under $10/month. Custom wins when you want it to be exactly right, load fast, and stop costing you money every month. The trade-off is maintenance: a custom section becomes yours to look after.

**Will I be able to edit it myself?**
That's the whole point. Every text, image, colour and layout choice becomes a setting in your theme customizer. You get a one-page guide showing where each one is. If you find yourself needing to message me to change a word, I built it wrong.

**What if I don't like it?**
That's what the spec is for. Before I write any code, you get a written document describing exactly what it will do, what you'll be able to change, and what happens in edge cases. You approve it first. That's why revisions are rarely needed.

**Will it slow down my store?**
It renders server-side and loads no external scripts, so it's typically faster than the app equivalent. On the Premium package I measure before and after and show you the numbers.

**Will it survive a theme update?**
Custom code lives in your theme files, so a theme update can overwrite it. Before updating, duplicate your theme and check — I can also handle the update for you. This is true of any custom work, including anything a previous developer added.

**Can you build [complex feature]?**
Tell me what it does. If it needs server-side logic, checkout changes, or writing data back to Shopify, it needs an app rather than a theme section — and I'll say so before you order rather than after.

**My theme is heavily customised. Is that a problem?**
Usually not, but send me the theme name first. Heavily modified themes take longer, and I'd rather quote correctly than discover it mid-build.
