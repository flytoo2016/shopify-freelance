## FAQ

**How long does a migration take?**
For a small store, one to two weeks. For a mid-size WooCommerce store, three to five weeks. Anyone quoting three days is transferring data and skipping redirects, SEO metadata, configuration and validation — which is where the actual work is.

**Will I lose my Google rankings?**
Not if the redirects are complete. But expect a dip: your organic traffic will typically drop between weeks 2 and 6 while Google recrawls and reindexes your entire site, then recover around month 3. I'll send you a weekly report through that period so you're not watching it blind.

**Can you migrate my customer passwords?**
No — and neither can anyone else. Every platform stores passwords as irreversible hashes. Your customers will each set a new password on first login. I plan a communication campaign around this so it doesn't overwhelm your support.

**What about my subscriptions?**
Active subscriptions don't migrate. Subscribers have to be re-onboarded through a Shopify subscription app, usually with new payment authorisation. It's a separate project and it needs to start weeks before the switch, not days.

**Will my new store look the same?**
It will be equivalent in function, not identical in appearance. WooCommerce themes are PHP; Shopify uses Liquid. The theme gets rebuilt. If matching a specific design matters to you, that's design work quoted separately.

**Will my store go down?**
Your current store runs normally throughout. The only moment anything changes is the DNS switch, which typically takes minutes. You get a written rollback plan that restores your old store if something goes seriously wrong.

**Why is your audit paid when others quote for free?**
Because a free quote is a guess. The audit is real work: crawling every URL, inventorying your catalogue, checking every plugin against Shopify's actual capabilities. You keep the document either way, and the fee is deducted if you go ahead with me.

**What if the audit says I shouldn't migrate?**
Then I'll say so, and you'll have saved yourself several thousand euros. It happens.
