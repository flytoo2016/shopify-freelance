## Requirements

```
1. Store URL (and storefront password if password-protected)
2. Read-only Shopify collaborator access, limited to Themes, Apps and Reports.
   I don't need access to orders, customers or finances.
3. Google Analytics 4 read access, if you use it
4. Search Console read access, if you have it
5. Your 3 best-selling product pages
6. Your current conversion rate and mobile/desktop split
7. 3-5 competitors or stores you admire, with URLs
8. What questions do customers ask you most often before buying? (This is the
   single most useful thing you can send me — it's the list of objections your
   product page isn't answering.)
9. What are your most common reasons for returns or cancellations?
10. Roughly what budget do you have for implementing fixes? This isn't about my
    price, which is fixed — it's so my roadmap contains things you can actually do.
11. Anything I shouldn't recommend changing?
```

Les questions 8 et 9 sont ton avantage concurrentiel. Aucun audit automatique ne les pose, et elles produisent les constats les plus spécifiques du rapport.

## FAQ

**Will this increase my conversion rate?**
The audit itself won't change anything — implementing it might. I won't quote you a percentage, because anyone who does is guessing. What I give you is an ordered list of what to fix first, with evidence for each item and an honest note about which conclusions are certain and which are hypotheses.

**How is this different from the cheap audits on this platform?**
Those are generated from a checklist. Mine is based on your analytics, a recorded journey through your store on a real phone, and your theme's actual code. Every finding cites a screenshot, a number, or a file and line. Send me a message and I'll show you a sample page.

**Do you need admin access?**
Read-only, and limited: Themes, Apps and Reports. Nothing about your orders, customers or money. You can revoke it any time and I'll remind you to at the end.

**What if I can't give you analytics access?**
I can still audit what's observable, but I'll say so on page one. Without your data I can tell you what *might* be losing you sales; with it I can tell you what *is*, and at which step. It's a real difference in the value of the report.

**Can you implement the fixes?**
Yes, quoted separately. The roadmap doubles as a spec — you can also hand it to your own developer or agency, which is why I write the recommendations precisely enough for someone else to execute.

**My store is new / has little traffic. Is this useful?**
Probably not yet, and I'd rather tell you now. Below roughly 1,000 sessions a month there isn't enough data to distinguish a real problem from noise, and most of the report would be assumption. Message me with your numbers and I'll tell you honestly.

**Will you tell me my store is bad?**
I'll tell you what I find, including what's already working — there's a dedicated section for that. The report is written to be used, not to impress or to alarm.
