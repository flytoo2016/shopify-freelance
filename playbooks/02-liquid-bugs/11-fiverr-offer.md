## Description

```
SOMETHING ON YOUR SHOPIFY STORE STOPPED WORKING?

Cart button doing nothing. Variants not updating the price. A section showing
"This section is not available". Everything broken since a theme update.
Errors in the console you can't read.

I find the actual cause, fix it, and tell you what happened in plain language.

HOW I WORK
1. I reproduce the problem myself. If I can't reproduce it, I tell you before
   you pay — I don't guess.
2. I isolate it: is it your theme, an app, your data, or the browser? This step

3. I trace it to the exact line.
4. I fix it on a duplicate, unpublished theme. Your live store is untouched.
5. I test the full checkout flow, not just the thing that was broken.
6. I explain the root cause so you know what happened and how to avoid it.

WHAT I FIX
✔ Liquid errors, broken sections, missing snippets
✔ Cart bugs — add to cart, drawer, quantities, AJAX errors, multi-market issues
✔ Product page — variants, price, images, availability, add-to-cart form
✔ Collection pages — filters, sorting, pagination, missing products
✔ JavaScript errors and console errors
✔ Mobile and responsive display issues
✔ Sections broken or unavailable in the theme editor
✔ Schema and JSON template errors
✔ Metafields and dynamic sources not displaying
✔ App conflicts (I identify the cause and work around it in the theme)
✔ Regressions after a theme update

WHAT I DON'T DO
✘ Build features that never existed — that's development, not a bug fix
✘ Edit third-party app code (nobody can — it's not in your theme)
✘ Uninstall apps without your decision
✘ Touch your live theme without written approval
✘ Promise a fix before I've reproduced the problem

WHY THE ISOLATION STEP MATTERS
A large share of "theme bugs" are not in the theme. They're app conflicts,
missing data, or leftovers from a previous fix. Someone who starts editing code
immediately will change things that were never the problem — and you'll pay for
it twice.

Send me your store URL, the page, and what you're doing when it breaks. I'll
tell you what I think is happening before you order.
```

## FAQ

**How fast can you fix it?**
I'll tell you after I reproduce it — usually within a couple of hours of getting access. I don't promise a deadline before I've seen the problem, because a bug that looks identical from the outside can take twenty minutes or two days depending on its cause.

**Will you touch my live store?**
No. I duplicate your theme first as a dated backup, then work on an unpublished copy. You review a preview link and approve in writing before anything is published. You also get a rollback plan.

**What if the bug is caused by an app, not the theme?**
That happens often. I'll tell you exactly which app, which file, and what to send their support team. Where possible I can work around it in the theme — I'll always tell you clearly when something is a workaround rather than a real fix, because a workaround can stop working when the app updates.

**What if you can't reproduce the problem?**
I'll tell you before you pay, and help you gather what's needed to reproduce it. I won't apply a fix to a problem I haven't seen — that's how people end up paying for changes that fix nothing.

**Do you need full admin access?**
No. The Theme Access app gives me theme files only. A collaborator account limited to Themes and Apps is useful because app conflicts are a common cause, but it's optional.

**My theme was customised by a previous developer. Can you still work on it?**
Yes. It may take longer if there's no version history — I'll tell you after looking.

**Everything broke after I updated my theme. Can you restore it?**
If your previous theme is still in your theme library, yes — I can compare both versions and reapply your customisations one at a time. If it was deleted, it becomes a rebuild rather than a restore, which is a different amount of work. Check your theme library before ordering.

**Is the fix guaranteed?**
The bug I fix is guaranteed for the period in your package. That covers the same problem coming back on its own. It doesn't cover new bugs, changes made by someone else, or newly installed apps.

**Can you also add [feature]?**
That's development, not a bug fix — different work, different quote. Happy to discuss it separately.
